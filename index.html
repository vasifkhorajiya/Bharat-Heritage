India is not one image.
It is a system of many worlds.

To experience the country deeply is to move between scales: a mountain passes into a city; a city into a courtyard; a courtyard into a kitchen; a gesture into a story.

This digital atlas follows those transitions instead of flattening them.

25 / DEEP TIME — EXTENDED

A longer timeline
than any interface.

c. 2500 BCE

Urban foundations

Planned settlements, craft production, water systems and long-distance exchange become evidence of complex urban life.

c. 1500–500 BCE

New cultural vocabularies

Language, ritual, agriculture and social organisation change through migrations, exchanges and the development of new traditions.

c. 600 BCE–500 CE

Philosophy, cities, mathematics

Multiple intellectual, artistic and political traditions grow across connected regions.

c. 700–1700

Networks of exchange

Ports, courts, pilgrimage routes and devotional movements create new artistic and commercial ecosystems.

1700–1947

Colonial modernity

New infrastructures, institutions and economic systems transform cities, trade, education and political imagination.

1947–NOW

A republic in motion

Industry, democracy, migration, urbanisation, media and digital technology keep reshaping everyday life.
